# V-Care Hospital — Final Pricing Strategy

## Chosen Pricing Model
**High Setup + SaaS Model with Negotiation Buffer**

### 1. The Numbers
- **Initial Quoted Setup Fee:** ₹50,000
- **Negotiated Target Setup Fee:** ₹30,000
- **Monthly SaaS/Cloud Fee:** ₹2,000 / month

### 2. The Strategy (Why This Works)
- **Price Anchoring:** By starting at ₹50,000, you establish the software as a premium product. When you let them negotiate it down to ₹30,000, they feel like they won a massive victory, while you still walk away with your actual target price.
- **Profitable Recurring Revenue:** The ₹2,000/month fee is low enough to be a no-brainer for a hospital's operating budget, but still gives you a fantastic profit margin (costing you only ~₹500/month for Railway/Supabase). This nets you ₹24,000 of passive income annually.

### 3. Your Pitch / Value Justification
If they ask what the monthly fee covers or why it's not a one-time purchase:
> *"The ₹2,000 monthly fee is what keeps your system alive and automated. It covers the secure cloud servers hosting your database, continuous uptime monitoring, daily backups, and ongoing access to the mobile app dashboard. Unlike a regular offline machine that requires manual data entry and USB drives, you are paying a tiny fraction of an HR person's salary to have your attendance fully automated in the cloud."*
