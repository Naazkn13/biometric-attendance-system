# Ashu Clinic — Enterprise Pricing Strategy

Unlike V-Care (which is a stripped-down, simplified punch-log viewer), **Ashu Clinic** is receiving the fully-featured, enterprise-grade HR suite that you originally developed. 

## 1. Value Breakdown (Why Ashu Clinic Costs More)

You are not just providing attendance logging; you are providing a full **Human Resources Information System (HRIS)**. 

Your original codebase for them includes:
- **Payroll Calculation:** Automatically tracking LOP (Loss of Pay) and working days.
- **Leave Management:** Handing leave requests, Maker/Checker approvals, and rejections.
- **Shift Master Logic:** Handling complex working hours, late marks, and break deductions.
- **Manual Overrides & Corrections:** Admin controls to fix missed punches securely.

You are effectively replacing the need for them to buy expensive software like Zoho People, Keka HR, or hiring a dedicated HR data-entry clerk.

---

## 2. Recommended Pricing Model

Because of the massive value of the automated payroll and leave management, the price must reflect a full ERP/HRMS rollout.

### The Numbers (Suggested)
- **Initial Quoted Setup Fee:** ₹1,20,000
- **Negotiated Target Setup Fee:** ₹80,000 to ₹90,000
- **Monthly SaaS/Cloud Fee:** ₹5,000 to ₹7,000 / month

### 3. The Strategy & Justification

- **High Value Anchoring:** Start the quote at ₹1.2 Lakhs. This establishes that they are getting a complete, custom enterprise system, not an off-the-shelf basic app. 
- **Higher Monthly Fee:** A system that processes payroll requires a more robust database, higher security, and more potential support tickets from them. ₹5,000/month is incredibly cheap for an HR system that automatically calculates salaries and handles leave.
- **The Pitch:** 
  > *"This system does the work of a full-time HR assistant. It automatically calculates shift deductions, manages leave approvals securely through a Maker/Checker workflow, and prepares your payroll data accurately without human error. A dedicated HR clerk costs ₹20,000+ per month. We are completely automating that workflow for a one-time setup of ₹1.2L and just ₹5,000 a month to keep the secure cloud and payroll calculations running."*
